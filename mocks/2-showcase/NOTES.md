# Direction 2: Showcase

Dark and cinematic, like a game storefront and a game's own character-creation screen. The art is the only colour; the interface is dusk.

## Palette
- Dusk `#1b1630` page (the pixel art's own shadow colour)
- Well `#130f24` where art stands (one step below the page)
- Rise `#241d3b` bars and panels; Lift `#2f2749` controls; Line `#3a3157` hairlines
- Ink `#f4eee2` text; Ink 2 `#b9afc9` quiet text; Ink 3 `#9187a8` quietest (still 4.5:1)
- Coin gold `#f3c453`: the one accent, for the primary action and for "this is selected"

## Type
Unbounded 500 to 600 for the few large headlines and the big numbers only. Figtree for everything else.
Pixelify Sans for the wordmark only. Spacing scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, 112.

## How it answers the complaints
- "Messy, elements everywhere": no outlines, no drop shadows, no pattern. Two surface steps and one accent. Cards have no frame: a dark well, a name, one tag.
- "Nothing moves, nothing shows the art can change": the landing opens on a real scene made of sprites. One press of a look restyles all of them; one press of a kit swaps the world.
- "Fifty filter rows before a result": four drop-downs in one row, each showing counts. With no search the page is curated shelves; with one it is a plain grid.
- "Scroll away from the picture to change the cape": the remix page never scrolls. The Hero stays in the middle; a tab on the left swaps the options on the right. All 20 controls sit in 7 tabs, plus Look and Sounds.
- "Too many clicks, changes not kept": every change saves itself and the top bar says "Saved in your bag". Changing an asset is what puts it in the bag. "In a forest" tries the Hero on a real background without leaving the page. From the library, "Change it" is one click.
- Choices are pictures: each hair style, headgear, weapon, cape and look is a small live Hero wearing it, with your other choices kept.

## Bag and playground (not mocked)
The bag would be the same screen as the remix page with a different centre: a shelf of what you picked down the left (small live pictures instead of category tabs), the selected thing large on the stage, its options on the right, and Download for the whole bag in the top bar, so changing something in the bag is the same gesture as on its own page. The playground would be the landing scene made playable: the full-width scene with the kit and look switches sitting on it, your bag as a filmstrip along the bottom to drop things in, and the same right-hand panel sliding in when you press a sprite, so a change made while playing is kept without leaving.
