Make your own

    From library to game in four steps

    1. Pick

      Search in plain words, or take a whole kit that already belongs together.

    2. Change

      Choose from pictures and move a few sliders. Every change is kept in your bag.

    3. Try

      Put what you picked into a small playable scene and watch it move.

    4. Download

      One zip of sprite sheets and sounds, ready for your game.

    Three complete kits

    Each one is a whole small game's worth: characters, enemies, ground, pickups, effects, menus, sounds and music made to go together.

    Sounds and music come with it

    56 sounds and 6 music tracks, made the same way as the art. Pick a style, change the length or the lead instrument. Each animation already knows which sounds it plays, and when.

      Works in Unity, and in any other engine

      A download is plain files: sprite sheets as PNG with a small data file beside each, and sounds and music as WAV. If your engine reads PNG and WAV, it reads Loadout. A Unity package is coming later.

      What is in the zip

      • Sprite sheetsPNG, at 1x, 2x or 4x pixel size
      • A data file per sheetframe sizes and animation names
      • Sounds and musicWAV
