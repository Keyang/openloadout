Sounds and music are included

56 sounds and 6 music tracks, made the same way as the art, so they can change too.

  • Sounds have styles. A sword slash can be a heavy blade or a quick dagger.
  • Music fits your level. Make a track shorter or longer and choose its lead instrument.
  • Characters know their sounds. The Hero's footsteps land on the right frame of the walk.
