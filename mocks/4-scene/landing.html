From nothing to a game you can see, in four steps

You do not draw, record or write code. You choose, you nudge a few sliders, and what you chose stands in one scene the whole time, so nothing you change gets lost.

  1. Pick

    Take a hero, enemies, a place, sounds and music from the library, or start from a whole kit.

  2. Change

    Hair, weapon, height, colours, how it moves. The Hero alone has 20 things to change and 9 animations.

  3. Try

    Everything you pick lands in your game scene next to the rest, so you see what fits before you build anything.

Three complete kits, each one a whole small game

A kit is sprites, sounds and music made to belong together. Start from one and restyle all of it at once with a look, such as Sunset, Frost or Night.

Sounds and music come with it, already in step

The Hero's footsteps land on the frames where the boots touch the ground. Every sound has styles too: a jump can be a tiny hop or a big leap, and each track has calmer and busier versions.

    Works in Unity and in any engine that reads PNG and WAV
